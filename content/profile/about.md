# About Mohd Afsar

Mohd Afsar (commonly known as **Afsar**) is a software and AI engineer currently pursuing his Bachelor of Science in Data Science and Applications at the **Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras)** (2023–2027).

He focuses on building **intelligent agentic systems**, **privacy-preserving AI architectures**, and **scalable full-stack applications**. He pairs a strong foundation in core Computer Science—such as Data Structures, Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming, and Database Management Systems—with modern engineering practices across TypeScript, Python, and modern web frameworks.

## Core Technical Focus
- **Agentic AI & Browser Systems**: Engineering lightweight on-device visual perception and action-planning agents (notably demonstrated in the ISRO Smart India Hackathon project *RedactVision Agent*).
- **Full-Stack Scientific Software**: Designing high-throughput, interactive visualization and alignment interfaces for neuroimaging and histology workflows at the Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre (SGBC), IIT Madras.
- **Data Engineering & Applied Machine Learning**: Building resilient backend services, statistical analysis pipelines, and neural network workflows with PyTorch, Pandas, and PostgreSQL.

## Philosophy
Afsar believes that AI applications must be grounded, private-by-design, and tightly coupled with intuitive, responsive user interfaces. Rather than building chatbots that guess or hallucinate, he designs deterministic guardrails, structured tool invocation protocols, and verifiable data pipelines.
